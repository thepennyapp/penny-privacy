
penny privacy policy
Last updated: October 19, 2026

penny is a spending check-in app for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: penny doesn't collect any of your data.

What penny stores, and where
Everything you enter in penny stays on your iPhone:

the spends you log (their range, any exact amount, how they felt, and any note)
days you mark as no-spend days
your settings, such as currency, appearance, theme and reminders
This information is stored in penny's private storage on your device, shared only with penny's own home-screen widget. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. penny has no accounts, no servers and no sign-in.

What penny doesn't do
penny does not collect, sell or share personal information.
penny does not link to your bank or read your financial accounts.
penny contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
penny does not track you across other apps or websites.

Insights and reports
Insights, the year in review and the PDF reports are all worked out on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded to do this.

Exports and sharing
When you export a CSV or PDF, or share your year image, penny hands the file to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there (Messages, Files, Photos or another app) is your choice, and is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

If you choose Save Image, penny asks for permission to add the image to your photo library. penny can only add images; it can't see or read your photos.

Reminders
If you turn on reminders, penny schedules them on your iPhone using iOS notifications. They aren't sent from a server, and nothing about them leaves your device.

Purchases
penny+ is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; penny only receives confirmation of whether penny+ is active. penny never sees your payment details. See Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data
You can delete everything penny has stored at any time in Settings → reset all data, or by deleting the app. Because penny keeps no copy, deleted data can't be recovered.

Children
penny is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
